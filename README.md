This Task focused on developing a machine learning model to predict product sales based on advertising expenditure across three marketing channels: Television (TV), Radio, and Newspaper. Using a dataset of 200 advertising campaigns, the analysis followed a structured data science workflow that included data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), feature selection, model training, prediction, and performance evaluation. The primary objective was to understand how advertising investments influence sales outcomes and to build a predictive model that can support data-driven marketing decisions.

The analysis demonstrated that advertising spending has a strong influence on sales performance, with TV advertising emerging as the most influential factor, followed by Radio advertising, while Newspaper advertising contributed very little to sales. A Linear Regression model achieved an excellent R² score of 0.899, indicating that approximately 90% of the variation in sales can be explained by the advertising expenditures included in the model. Additionally, advertising budget simulations showed that increasing TV advertising expenditure consistently increases predicted sales, highlighting the importance of strategic investment in high-performing marketing channels.
